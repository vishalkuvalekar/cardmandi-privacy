CardMandi Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Applies to the CardMandi app for Android (com.cardmandi.app)

CardMandi is a collection of traditional Indian card games, published by Vishal Kuvalekar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who it is shared with. CardMandi has no accounts or servers of its own: your profile, game record and settings are stored only on your phone.

In short:

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not run any server that receives it.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • If you leave "Usage data" switched on, anonymous usage statistics are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy.
  • When you play with nearby phones, your display name and game moves go directly to those phones and nowhere else.

1. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded to us:

  • Your profile: the display name you choose (or "You" by default) and, if you sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games player name and ID.
  • Your game record: matches played and won, per game.
  • Your settings: card deck and table styles, game options, reminder choices and your "Usage data" choice.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this.

2. Information collected by third parties

Advertising: Google AdMob

CardMandi is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID / app set ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) is inferred
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. If consent is not given, only non-personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices. Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

If the Usage data switch is on (it is on by default), the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase. These help us understand which games are played and how the app is used, so we can improve it. The events cover:

  • which screens and games are opened, and for how long
  • rounds and matches finished, and whether you won
  • how many ads were shown in a session, and whether a reward video was watched
  • whether you play as a guest or signed in with Play Games (the type only, not your account)

We never send your name, Play Games ID or cards. Firebase also records an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate region derived from your IP address. Turning Usage data off in Settings → Privacy stops collection completely on that device. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Sign-in: Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player name and player ID with the app. We use them only to label your profile on your phone. Playing as a guest requires no sign-in. Google's handling of your Play Games account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Playing with nearby phones

To play with people in the same room, CardMandi uses Google Nearby Connections, which works over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. It sends data directly between the phones at the table and does not use the internet or any server. The data it sends is:

  • your display name, so the other players can see who has joined
  • game moves and table state, limited so that no phone receives another player's hidden cards

Android asks for the Nearby devices permission (on Android 12 and later) or the Bluetooth and location permissions (on Android 11 and earlier) to make these connections. On older Android versions this is required because Android treats Bluetooth scanning as a location permission. CardMandi never reads, stores or sends your location. You can deny these permissions and still play every game on your own against computer players.

4. Notifications

If you turn on reminders in Settings, the app schedules them on your phone. No data is sent anywhere to do this, and reminders are off by default.

5. Sharing an invite

The "invite" button opens Android's share sheet with a link to CardMandi on Google Play. The app does not see who you share it with.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months and then deletes it automatically. AdMob data is kept according to Google's own policies. Because the analytics data is not linked to your name or account, we usually cannot pick out an individual's records. If you would like us to try to delete data associated with your device, email us at the address below and we will do what Google's tools allow.

8. Children

CardMandi is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EEA, the UK, California or India (under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Most of these rights you can exercise directly in the app, using the Usage data switch, Ad choices and the profile name in Settings, or by clearing the app's data. For anything else, email us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
